How to descale a kettle

Limescale builds up wherever hard water is boiled. It is harmless, and it slows the kettle down.

  1. Fill the kettle half way with equal parts water and white vinegar.
  2. Bring it to the boil, switch it off and leave it for 30 minutes.
  3. Pour it out, then boil fresh water twice and discard it.

Repeat every two months in a hard water area.
